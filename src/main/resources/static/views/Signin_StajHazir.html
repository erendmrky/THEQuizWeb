Aptitude Test is an assessment used to measure a person's natural ability to learn or perform in specific areas or tasks. It typically consists of four sections:

Verbal: Assesses language, grammar, and reading comprehension skills.

Non-verbal: Tests visual reasoning through patterns, shapes, and diagrams.

Numerical: Measures ability to work with numbers and solve mathematical problems.

Mechanical: Evaluates understanding of mechanical concepts and physical principles.
